# Copy

## Buttons and actions

Name the action: “Send invite”, “Start free board”, “View pricing”. Not “Submit”, “Get Started Free”, “Learn more” as a costume pair.

Paid plans need paid-sounding CTAs: “Start Team trial”, “Get Pro”, “Get Business”, with price or trial context nearby. Avoid a bare “Start” on a paid tier.

## Headlines and labels

- No eyebrow / pill / “New ·” kicker above a heading.
- Prefer short concrete lines. Same size as body is fine; weight does the work.
- Prefer sentence-case section labels (“Selected work”) over tiny tracked uppercase ornament.

## Brand names

Invent neutral product names that do not collide with existing AI models or famous products. Do not use names like Muse, Claude, Copilot, Gemini as example brands.

## Voice

- No stock AI phrases (“unlock the future”, “seamless”, “supercharge”, “delve”, “in today’s fast-paced…”).
- **No em dashes** in UI copy or README-facing prose. Use a period, comma, or parentheses.
- No fake proof. Keep real product words on redesigns unless asked to rewrite.

## Lists

Plain language and position over “01 / 02 / 03” costume numbering.

## Word budgets per slot
H1 2–8 words (aim for 4). H2 1–7 (aim for 4). Feature or card title 1–4 (aim for 2). Lead 7–19 words (aim for 13). CTA 1–4 words (aim for 2), a verb plus an object. If a slot runs over budget, cut words; do not shrink the type.
