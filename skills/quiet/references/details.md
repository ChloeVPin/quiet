# Interaction details

Skip any item that does not apply. When it applies, do it.

## Emil checklist

- **Press:** scale interactive controls to ~**0.97** on `:active` (~100–150ms, ease-out).
- **Origin-aware popovers:** `transform-origin` at the trigger. Center origin is usually wrong.
- **Never `scale(0)`:** enter from ~**0.9–0.95** with opacity.
- **Subsequent tooltips:** once one is open, skip delay on the next.
- Hit targets: prefer **≥44px** via padding or pseudo without growing visuals.

## Continuity

- Interruptible transitions. Freeze exiting content. Morph icons instead of crossfades.
- Spatial consistency: enter from / return to where the thing lives.

## Specimen craft (Rauno / Devouring Details)

When showing design (README, docs, marketing craft moments):

- Put the control on a quiet **stage**, oversized enough to read the joinery.
- Annotate sparingly with a craft accent (measure ticks, radii math like `10 = 6 + 4`, timing).
- Prefer one frozen or live interaction over a paragraph of claims.
- Accent color is instrumentation, not a brand gradient.

## Feel

- Hover the child, not a jittery parent.
- Filterable menus when lists are long.
- Native affordances when the platform already solves it.
