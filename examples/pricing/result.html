Questions

Can we change plans later?
Yes. Upgrades apply right away and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at renewal.
What happens after the trial?
You pick a plan or stay on Free. We never charge a card you did not add.
Do you bill per active seat?
Yes. Seats that have not opened Fieldkit in 30 days are not billed.
Do you offer nonprofit pricing?
Team is 50% off for registered nonprofits. Email billing with your details.
