Cycles 2.0

Cycle goals, carry-over, and a quieter progress strip. Existing cycles migrate automatically.

What changed

  • Each cycle can name a goal in one line of text.
  • Unfinished issues can carry to the next cycle with one click.
  • Progress uses a single thin bar instead of a card grid.

Migration

Open cycles keep their dates and membership. Goals start empty. Nothing is deleted.
