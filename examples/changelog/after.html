Oct 9, 2026Version 4.1.3

Faster issue search

Search across titles and ids is about twice as fast on workspaces with more than 10,000 issues.

  • Prefix matching on ids, so PLT-5 finds PLT-512 as you type.
  • Recent first. Results you opened this week rank higher.
  • Fixed search ignoring archived projects when the filter was on.
Oct 3, 2026Version 4.1.2

Due dates use your timezone

Due dates stored in UTC now render in the timezone on your profile, so an issue due Friday no longer shows Thursday evening on the West Coast.

Sep 28, 2026Version 4.1.1

Assign an owner from the keyboard

Press O on any issue to open the owner picker. Type to filter, Enter to assign, Esc to dismiss.
