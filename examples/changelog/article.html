Cycle goals

Give each cycle one sentence that says what done looks like. The goal sits in the list header, appears in the weekly digest, and is the first thing people see when they open the cycle.

Carry-over with a reason

When a cycle ends, open issues move to the next one automatically. Cascade asks for a short reason, such as blocked on review or scope grew, and shows it beside the issue so nobody has to ask in standup.

  • Bulk carry-over. Select several issues and press M to move them together.
  • Carry-over history. An issue that moved three cycles in a row gets flagged in the cycle summary.

Progress in the header

The old progress strip is gone. The header now shows percent complete and issues done, both updated live as work moves.

Migration

Existing cycles migrate on their own and keep their dates. Goals start empty, and admins can add them from cycle settings.
