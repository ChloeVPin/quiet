Sparse attention in long documents

Across the four corpora, the sparse variant matched dense attention within 0.4 points while using 38% of the memory at 32k tokens. The gap widened on legal filings, where long-range references are frequent.

We attribute most of the remaining error to cross-reference resolution, which the windowed heads handle poorly beyond 8k tokens.
