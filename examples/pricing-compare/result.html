Billing questions

Still unsure? Our team answers billing email within one business day.

What counts as a crew?

A crew is one truck or team that appears as a row on the board. Techs inside a crew are free.

Can I change plans mid-year?

Yes. Upgrades are prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.

Do you charge for seasonal crews?

Pause a crew during the off season and it drops off your bill the next month.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing is charged until you add a card. Your board and history stay put for 60 days.
